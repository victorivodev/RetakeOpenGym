# Exercise media: not included in Retake Gym

openGym's exercise stills and animations are licensed to openGym by Gym visual (gymvisual.com) and
may not be reused by third parties, so this fork ships without them. The app shows the exercise
name, instructions and the muscle map instead.

To add media back, license it from Gym visual or use a source whose licence allows it, and place
the files in `still/` (.webp) and `clip/` (.mp4) here.
